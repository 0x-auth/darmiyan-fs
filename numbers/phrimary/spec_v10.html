Phri-mary Number System

Three primitives: two digits under one collapse rule, two readings of the same code, and a chosen resolution. Integers, primes, unique factorization, arithmetic, periods and the 0-to-1 outside view all emerge from them; none is put in.

Infinity is a choice of resolution. Choose Δx, and largeness outward and depth inward are the same number of ticks; a prime is the smallest cluster of indivisible codes whose two readings agree.

Primitives

1 · CODE

Digits 0 and 1. Adjacent 1s collapse upward.

011 → 100 0200 → 1001
2 · TWO READINGS

The same digits, read with two place values.

inside x = Σ dᵢ Φⁱ outside x′ = Σ dᵢ ψⁱ, ψ = −1/Φ
3 · RESOLUTION

Depth is chosen. Infinity is letting k grow.

Δx = Φ^(−k)

Both collapse rules preserve both readings. Collapse is unique whatever the rule order (0 of 2,000 strings depended on order); every 16-bit string collapses to the greedy code.

Inside and outside

ticks θ = ½ log_Φ(|x| / |x′|) fuzz γ = 2√|x·x′| / (|x| + |x′|) = sech(θ ln Φ) ∈ (0, 1] spiral ψ^θ = Φ^(−θ) · e^(iπθ)
  • Integers sit at θ = 0, γ = 1 at any size: 1, 137 and 50,000 look alike from outside. TESTED
  • Largeness outward = depth inward: n reaches Φ^(log_Φ n) and Φ^(−log_Φ n); reading the code through the mirror returns n for all 2,000 integers. TESTED
  • Zero and infinity are one point outside: θ → ±∞ both give γ → 0. Infinity is the zero cell turned inside out: 1/[0, Δx] = [Φᵏ, ∞). TESTED
  • Each tick is a scale by Φ and a half-turn. The alternating errors are the turn; the radius gives direction. π enters as the rotation. TESTED
  • π drifts off balance about half a tick per digit: γ = 2.7 × 10⁻⁸ at depth 80. As a rotation it is ½ turn, code 0.(010). TESTED
  • v4's "Lorentz factor" 2/(Φ^θ + Φ^−θ) is this γ: the outside view of a number. TESTED

TD and TrD

FTA splits every code into two orthogonal parts. Map x to (log|x|, log|x′|); the diagonal and anti-diagonal directions are perpendicular, and both coordinates add under multiplication.

TD = (log|x| + log|x′|)/√2 storage: what both readings share (log mass) TrD = (log|x| − log|x′|)/√2 reference: how the readings differ (ticks)

Additivity holds to 8×10⁻¹⁰ over 20,000 random products. Units fill the TrD axis in steps of √2·lnΦ and never touch TD (Dirichlet's unit theorem). TD² + TrD² is only the length; the content is the perpendicular split.

Mass and the light cone

E = (x + x′)/2, p = (x − x′)/2, m² = x·x′ = E² − p²
  • Inside and outside are light-cone coordinates. γ = 2√|N|/(|x|+|x′|) equals dτ/dt to 10⁻¹⁵; each Φ² tick is a boost (rapidity 0.9624, v = 0.745c). TESTED
  • Mass is the norm: invariant under boosts (m = 3.316625 for 3 + Φ across five boosts). TESTED
  • Mass gap: no nonzero code is massless; the lightest mass is 1, the units. TESTED
  • Allowed m²: 1, 4, 5, 9, 11, 16, 19, 20, 25, 29, 31…; forbidden: any number with a prime ≡ ±2 mod 5 to an odd power. TESTED
  • Adding codes combines bodies: combined mass ≥ sum of masses, 0 violations in 1,317 pairs. 3 + Φ and 4 − Φ moving apart combine into 7 at rest, more than 6.633. TESTED
  • Causality is the sign of x·x′; a single Φ tick flips it, so only Φ² is a physical boost. The squeeze trade-off at fixed x·x′ has the form of saturated uncertainty. TESTED
  • Gravity: nothing in the codes curves yet. OPEN

Zeta from outside

The inside hears the ordinary zeta zeros; the outside hears its own, set by the mod-5 character. The zeros found here sit on the critical line, as the generalised Riemann hypothesis predicts; this is a check, not a proof.

Limits

  • No finite depth holds every prime. Product of all primes at depth 12, plus 1, needs depth ≈ 1,026 and has new prime factors. TESTED
  • No finite machine recognises primes. Every prefix of every length needs its own state (233 of 233 at length 13). TESTED
  • No set of dimensions makes everything finite. Each added dimension rescues its own numbers (base ρ makes ρ finite and Φ infinite); finite codes are countable, the reals are not. PROOF
  • What is finite is the rule set. The few lines above generate every prime, as "add 1" generates every integer.
  • Cost. 1.44× the digits of binary for the same precision.
